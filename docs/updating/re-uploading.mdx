# Re-uploading

If you've made changes to your avatar — or updated your packages — you'll need to re-upload it to VRChat for those changes to show up in-game. The process is almost identical to the first upload.

:::info
Re-uploading overwrites the existing version of your avatar in VRChat. You don't end up with duplicates — it updates the same avatar in your list.
:::

---

## When do you need to re-upload?

You need to re-upload any time you've made a change inside Unity that you want to see in VRChat. For example:

- You updated a package (like GoGoLoco or VRCFury)
- You swapped out a texture or material
- You adjusted a setting on the avatar
- You made any other edit inside the Unity project

Simply updating packages in VCC is **not enough on its own** — you always need to re-upload after.

---

## How to re-upload

1. Open your project in Unity through the **Creator Companion**.
2. Make sure your avatar is in the scene. If it isn't, find it in the **Project** window and drag it into the **Hierarchy**.
3. Click **VRChat SDK** in the top menu bar, then **Show Control Panel**.
4. Go to the **Builder** tab and confirm your avatar is selected in the dropdown.
5. Check for any red errors — fix those before continuing.
6. Click **Build & Publish for Windows**.
7. Wait for Unity to finish building. This may take a few minutes.

That's it — your avatar in VRChat will now reflect the changes.

:::tip
You don't need to re-enter your avatar's name or description. VRChat remembers those from the first upload since the avatar is being updated, not created from scratch.
:::

---

## Re-uploading the Quest version

If your avatar has a Quest version, you'll need to re-upload that separately too. After completing the steps above, repeat them and click **Build & Publish for Android** instead of Windows.

The Quest and PC versions are linked by their shared ID, so you don't need to go through the ID-linking process again — that only needs to be done once on first upload.
