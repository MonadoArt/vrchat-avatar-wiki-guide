# Uploading an Avatar

Make sure you've completed the [Installation](./installation) steps before continuing. Outfits and customization are covered in the [Customizing](../customizing) section.

---

## 1. Open the SDK window

In Unity, go to the top menu bar and click **VRChat SDK → Show Control Panel**.

:::caution
Put an image of where to find it
:::

If you don't see this menu, the SDK probably isn't installed correctly - go back to the <VccIcon />Creator Companion and make sure the VRChat SDK package is added to your project.

---

## 2. Sign in

In the SDK window, go to the **Authentication** tab and sign in with your VRChat account. If prompted for a two-factor authentication code, check your email.

:::tip
Sign in with your **email address**, not your username. Username logins don't tend to work as reliably in the SDK.
:::

---

## 3. Select your avatar

1. Switch to the **Builder** tab at the top of the SDK window.
2. In the **Selected Avatar** dropdown, choose your avatar. If the dropdown is empty, make sure the avatar's scene is open and the avatar is in the Hierarchy.
3. The SDK will run a few checks. Any errors shown in red will have a button next to them to fix them automatically - click it and continue. Warnings in yellow are usually safe to ignore.

---

## 4. Upload (PCVR)

1. Fill in the **Avatar Name** and give it a **Thumbnail** - click **Select Image** to use an image file, or **Capture in Scene** to take a screenshot from the Scene view. Description and tags are optional.
2. Make sure **Build & Publish your Avatar Online** is selected as the Build Type and **Windows** is selected in the Platform dropdown.
3. Click **Build & Publish**.
4. Wait for Unity to finish building. This can take anywhere from a few minutes to upward of 20 - Unity may look frozen during this time, which is normal. Don't close it.
5. If successful, you'll see <q>Build Successful</q> in green text in the SDK panel.

Your avatar will appear in VRChat under **My Avatars**.

---

## 5. Upload (Mobile/Quest)

If you want Quest users to see your avatar when you're on PC (and vice versa), you need to upload a mobile version and link it to the same avatar slot.

:::info
**What's a Blueprint ID?**

It's VRChat's internal ID for an avatar slot. Linking both versions to the same ID means they count as one avatar. If you skip this, they'll be treated as two completely separate uploads and cross-platform visibility won't work.
:::

1. Find the prefab for the Mobile version and drag it into the Hierarchy.
2. In the SDK's **Content Manager** tab, find your uploaded PC avatar and click **Copy ID**.
3. In the Hierarchy, click the **top-level object of the Quest avatar** - the one at the very top, not any of the things nested inside it.
4. In the Inspector, scroll down until you see a section called **Pipeline Manager**.
5. Paste the copied ID into the **Blueprint ID** field and click **Attach**.
6. Repeat the upload steps above with **Android** selected in the Platform dropdown.

## Troubleshooting

If your build fails, the SDK throws an error you can't auto-fix, or the avatar doesn't appear in VRChat after uploading, check the [Troubleshooting](../troubleshooting) page for common errors and fixes.
