# Customizing Your Avatar

The avatar is built with VRCFury, which means it's modular - parts can be added, removed, and swapped out without needing to know 3D. Most of it is just dragging a prefab into Unity and re-uploading.

---

## Where do I start?

**I want to add an outfit, hair style, or system** - Go to [Add-ons](./add-ons). The process is the same for all of them.

**I want to repaint or retexture the avatar** - You have two routes depending on what software you use:
- [Substance Painter](./substance-painter) for 3D texture painting directly on the model
- [PSD & 2D Editing](./psd) if you prefer working flat in an image editor like Photoshop or Clip Studio Paint

**I want to apply a new texture I made** - [Materials & Textures](./materials-textures) covers swapping textures in Unity.

**I just uploaded - what's already in my radial menu?** - Everything is set up automatically on upload. Check the [Systems](./add-ons#systems) section for what's included out of the box.
