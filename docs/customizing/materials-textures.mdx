# Materials & Textures

If you want to change how the avatar looks beyond what the in-game color controls offer - like swapping a texture file entirely or adjusting the emission and roughness - you'll do that by editing the material directly in Unity.

---

## What's a material, what's a texture?

A **texture** is an image file - like a PNG - that gets painted onto the 3D model.

A **material** is the full set of settings that controls how a surface looks: which textures it uses, how shiny it is, whether it glows, and so on. The avatar body uses a single material, so there's one place to change all of this.

---

## Finding the material

1. In the **Project** panel, navigate to `Monado_Art/Lucario ReUnited VRC/Materials/`
2. Click the body material to select it
3. The **Inspector** will show all of its properties on the right

---

## What the slots do

| Slot | What it controls |
|---|---|
| **Base Color / Albedo** | The main painted texture |
| **Normal Map** | Surface detail like bumps and creases - usually leave this alone |
| **Emission** | Glow - color and texture of any glowing areas |
| **Metallic / Roughness** | How shiny or matte the surface looks |

---

## Swapping a texture

Drag your new texture file from the Project panel into the slot in the Inspector. Click the small thumbnail to the left of the slot name and it'll highlight - then drag your file onto it.

:::tip
Press **Ctrl+S** to save after making changes, then re-upload to see them in VRChat.
:::
